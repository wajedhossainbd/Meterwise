MeterWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

MeterWise is an offline-first app for recording prepaid electricity meter readings and splitting bills between tenants. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

Property name, meter details, tenant names and contact details, meter readings, recharge amounts, settlements, bill snapshots and any meter photos you take are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. They are not uploaded to any server operated by MeterWise. You can export a local zip backup from Settings → Backup and share it yourself if you choose.

Google account and Google Drive

If you enable Drive sync, the app asks for your Google account sign-in and permission to access Google Drive. The app uses that access only to:

  • create a folder named MeterWise/<meter code> in your own Drive;
  • read and write encrypted billing packets in that folder so a landlord and their tenants can synchronise data;
  • identify the signed-in account (email address) so you can see which account is connected in the app.

Packets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from the meter code. MeterWise has no server and never receives a copy of your Drive files. You can revoke the app's Drive access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

Permissions

  • Camera — photograph meters/receipts and scan a tenant QR code.
  • Internet / network state — Google sign-in and Drive sync only.
  • Notifications / boot / wake lock — the hourly background sync task; no promotional notifications.

Sharing and third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Invoice PDFs are generated on your device; sharing them is something you initiate through the system share sheet. Google's own privacy policy applies to Google sign-in and Drive.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Drive packets remain until you delete the MeterWise folder. Uninstalling the app does not delete files from your Drive.
