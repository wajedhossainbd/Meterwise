Fair prepaid electricity billing for multi-tenant buildings in Bangladesh.

## Full description

MeterWise is built for the way electricity is actually paid for in a shared
building: one prepaid main meter, several tenant sub-meters, and a landlord who
only wants to record readings and recharges.

WHAT IT DOES
- Records one reading session for the main meter and every tenant sub-meter
- Derives each tenant's consumption, including their share of common-area units
- Splits the official DPDC/BERC slab tariff fairly: every shared tenant gets an
  equal share of every slab; fixed-rate tenants are billed independently
- Tracks prepaid balances as a ledger, including recharges, tenant-to-tenant
  settlements and month-to-month carry-forward
- Produces clean, professional invoices in English and বাংলা (Bangla)
- Freezes finalized months as immutable snapshots so past bills never change

TENANT ACCESS
- The landlord shares a meter code or QR code
- Tenants join from their own phone, choose their name from the landlord's
  roster, and receive a private view of their own usage, bill, balance and
  recharge history
- Tenants can record their own sub-meter readings and recharges, and note manual
  payments or reimbursements

DRIVE SYNC (OPTIONAL)
- With the user's own Google account, encrypted packets are stored in their
  Google Drive — no MeterWise server, no subscription
- Landlord and tenant devices exchange data whenever they are online, with an
  hourly background sync

PRIVACY
- All billing data is stored locally on the device
- No ads, analytics or tracking
- Full privacy policy: https://wajedhossainbd.github.io/Meterwise/privacy.html
